Permanently redact PDF content

Permanently remove sensitive content by replacing marked pages with sanitized page images. Unmarked pages retain their original vector content.

Open Redact PDF

How to use this tool

  1. Open the PDF, draw black redaction boxes over sensitive areas, and apply the redactions.
  2. Wait for processing to finish in your browser.
  3. Download the generated file to your device.

Private PDF processing

AroundByte PDF processes supported files locally in your browser. Your PDF is not uploaded to an AroundByte server, and you do not need an account.

Limitations

Marked pages are flattened and their text is no longer selectable. Carefully inspect the output before sharing.
