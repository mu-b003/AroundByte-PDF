Rebuild a damaged PDF

Rebuild a PDF from pages that the browser PDF renderer can still open and display.

Open Repair PDF

How to use this tool

  1. Choose a PDF that can be opened partially, then rebuild it as a new PDF.
  2. Wait for processing to finish in your browser.
  3. Download the generated file to your device.

Private PDF processing

AroundByte PDF processes supported files locally in your browser. Your PDF is not uploaded to an AroundByte server, and you do not need an account.

Limitations

This method cannot repair files that PDF.js cannot open. Rebuilt pages are images, text is not selectable, and the current limit is 10 pages.
