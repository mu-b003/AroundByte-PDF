Protect a PDF online

Encrypt your PDF with AES-256, set an opening password and control printing, copying and modification permissions.

Open Protect PDF

How to use this tool

  1. Choose a PDF file and enter two different passwords.
  2. Choose which actions PDF readers should permit.
  3. Protect the PDF and download the encrypted copy.

Private PDF processing

Your PDF and passwords are processed locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to an AroundByte server, and no account is required.

Security note

AES-256 encryption protects access to the document. PDF permission restrictions depend on the reader and are not DRM; some third-party tools may bypass them. Keep your passwords safe.
